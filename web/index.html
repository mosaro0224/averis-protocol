01 LPs supply USDC to the vault.
02 A funded job names the Averis router as its immutable payout receiver.
03 The provider draws within an on-chain receivable cap (up to 40% of job budget).
04 Settlement repays principal + 2% fee; the remainder plus any unspent pool balance goes to the agent.
